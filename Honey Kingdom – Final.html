Our Process

Our bees forage freely across wild meadows, and we gather the harvest by hand. The honey is strained once and jarred raw, so it keeps the flavor of the flowers it came from.

We keep our hives small and spread them far apart, so every colony has its own patch of flowers. Each batch is tracked back to the hive it came from, and we never take more than the bees can spare for winter.

Questions

How can I tell honey is real?

Look for a clear origin and batch information, a floral aroma and a thick texture. Quick home tests are unreliable, so trust a seller who is open about the source.

Why did my honey turn solid?

That is crystallization, and it is natural in raw honey. Place the jar in warm water and stir gently to restore it.

How should I store it?

Keep the jar sealed at room temperature, away from direct sun. It does not need a fridge.

How much honey a day is fine?

One or two spoons is a common amount. It is still sugar, so enjoy it in moderation.

Is raw honey different from regular?

Raw honey is only lightly strained, with no high heat, so it keeps more of its natural flavor and pollen.

Can people with diabetes eat it?

Honey raises blood sugar, so ask a doctor before adding it to your diet.
